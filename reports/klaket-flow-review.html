Production flow review

Klaket preview · 6 Nov 2026 · one full cycle run on staging as “Flow test · Oasis Summer”, then a working redesign on branch design/slate-depth

Why it feels flat

  • Every step opens as a blank form or a wall of text. 10 free-text fields to start a production, 22 in the Brief, and a call sheet where the weather, location and crew call are typed by hand though the app already knows them.
  • AI waits look different on every screen and mostly show a still ring. The Brief needed a manual reload; Concepts ran for over 10 minutes with no sign of life.
  • Nothing says what to do next. Tabs, the phase menu and the overview cards are three copies of the same list.
  • Surfaces have no depth. Same-tone cards on the canvas, no elevation, and motion used as decoration (shine sweeps, bounce, pulsing dots) rather than to explain a change.

What the branch changes

  • New production is a 4-step wizard of tiles, chips and a tap-a-range calendar. A chalk slate fills in as you pick and claps when the production starts.
  • Bubble pickers everywhere: short dropdowns become one-tap chips, suggestion lists open as speech bubbles, brief fields get quick picks.
  • One agent card with named steps and a timer, which refreshes the screen itself when the work is in.
  • “Next up” on the overview, and a Slate style pack with real elevation and purposeful motion.

Recommendations

Starting a production

The first screen sets the tone for the whole job.

  • BuiltFour-step wizard: type as icon tiles, length and cutdowns as chips, recent clients and agencies as chips with a “New client” chip you can type into, a calendar where you tap the first and last shoot day (Fri/Sat marked), delivery as “2 weeks after”, and an auto title you can edit.
  • BuiltThe slate: a chalk clapperboard fills in line by line and claps shut on create. It’s the one big animated moment in the app. Lands on the Brief, not the Script.
  • BuiltStatus is set for you (Briefing), not picked from a dropdown. Production codes no longer repeat.
  • Next“Start from the client’s email” on step 1: paste once, create the production and run the Brief Agent together.

Less typing

Pick, don’t type, wherever the answer is one of a few.

  • BuiltShort dropdowns become one-tap chips in every form (any select with up to 7 short options). The real select stays underneath, so saving code is unchanged.
  • BuiltSuggestion lists open as speech bubbles (platform, duration, language, territory, department, location kind…), shaped like the Talking Slate.
  • BuiltQuick picks under Brief fields: tone, audience, music, KPI, revision rounds. One tap writes the word in, bilingual.
  • Built“Still missing” chips jump to the field, flash it and open it, and tick themselves off once it’s filled. Fields are smaller (36px) and text boxes grow with what you write.
  • NextLet the AI tell what a pasted source is (email, transcript, questionnaire) instead of asking in a dropdown of sentences.
  • NextDeliverables as cards built from chips (“Hero · 30s · 16:9 · TV”) instead of an 8-column table row.
  • NextCall sheet fills itself: crew call from the schedule, weather, sunrise/sunset and nearest hospital from the date and location.
  • LaterVoice-first brief on the phone: the memo button already exists; make it the main entry on mobile.

AI agents

The agents are the product’s best part. Their waits and results should feel like it.

  • BuiltOne “agent at work” card: the slate’s three red dots, the steps it’s on, elapsed time. It re-reads quietly every 8 s and redraws only when something changed, never while you type.
  • NextSame card for shots, schedule and breakdown, plus a “taking too long / try again” state with the real error.
  • NextConcepts as boards: title, one line, three frames, a budget meter and risk chips; the full write-up behind “Read the idea”.
  • NextBulk actions: accept breakdown tags by department; suggest shots for all scenes at once.

Knowing what’s next

Show where things stand before asking for input.

  • Built“Next up” on the overview: the one step waiting on you with a big button, the queue behind it, and what’s waiting on the client or an agent.
  • NextOne journey rail with done / your turn / waiting dots, replacing the tab strip, phase menu and overview cards (three copies of one list today).
  • NextA notice when an agent finishes while you’re on another screen.

Depth and motion

Same four colours and fonts; surfaces you can read at a glance.

  • Built“Slate” style pack (now the default in the preview dock): a deeper paper canvas, an elevation scale (sunken fields, resting cards, lifted on hover, floating bubbles and dialogs), lit top edges, pill tabs, buttons that press.
  • BuiltMotion that explains: bubbles grow from the control that opened them, picked chips check in, wizard steps slide in reading direction (mirrored in Arabic), screens rise in, skeletons instead of spinners. Reduce Motion keeps fades only.
  • BuiltClean-up: off-brand navy shadows replaced by an ink shadow token; 📋💡 emoji replaced by drawn icons; the shine sweep is off in Slate.
  • NextRetire the 23 bouncy curves and the decorative pulses in the remaining views.

Bugs found

From the run.

  • FixedCodes were counted, not incremented, so NF-2611 and NF-2612 exist twice.
  • FixedA new production opened Script and skipped the Brief.
  • FixedThe Brief didn’t refresh when its agent finished; toasts were hidden under the preview dock.
  • FixedThe agent’s “Still missing” list kept naming fields you had already filled.
  • OpenConcepts heading still says “internal until the brief is approved” after approval.
  • OpenThe schedule proposal pops up over unrelated screens; its crew call isn’t carried to the call sheet.
