## About (2,000 characters max)
Every production, one conversation.
كل إنتاج، في محادثة واحدة.

Klaket is the AI production platform built for Saudi and Gulf production houses. It brings the whole crew, from the producer and director to the heads of department and the client, into one place. A production runs there from the client's first brief all the way to shoot day.

Our mark is "The Talking Slate": a clapperboard that has become a speech bubble. The three dots mean someone is typing. The clap is the moment everyone hears "Action". That's the idea behind the product: production is a conversation, and it should live in one place, not across scattered emails, voice notes and spreadsheets.

What Klaket does:
🎬 Briefs that write themselves. A kickoff call, a client email or a voice memo becomes a structured brief in Arabic and English. Every value shows where it came from, and nothing counts until you approve it.
💡 Ideas, scripts and decks, in sync. AI agents propose safe, bold and wildcard creative routes. The script follows the chosen idea, and the treatment deck arrives with a frame for every shot.
📋 From script to schedule. An AI breakdown of every scene feeds the shot list, storyboard, stripboard, calendar, budget and bid.
✅ Clear client approvals. Clients see only what's been sent to them and approve the brief, treatment and bid in one tap, with a full audit trail.
🌍 Arabic and English, first class. Built in Saudi Arabia for how our industry actually works.

AI does the heavy lifting. People make the calls.

Ready? Action. جاهز؟ أكشن.

## Brand rules followed
- Four colours only: teal `#0F5C57` / `#0A3F3C`, ink `#121212`, paper `#F6EBD9`. Signal red `#F2483A` appears only in the mark's three dots.
- Cairo 900 headline, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, IBM Plex Mono for the slate label. The wordmark is never typed.
- The banner keeps its left third clear because LinkedIn places the logo over the bottom-left of the cover. The copy sits in the centre so mobile crops don't cut it.
- To regenerate: edit `banner.html` and re-render it with headless Chrome at `--window-size=1128,191 --force-device-scale-factor=2`.
